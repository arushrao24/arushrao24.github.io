Mixed Fastener Risk Identification

BMW Manufacturing

(No images for confidentiality purposes)

Overview

  • Investigated the risk of visually similar fasteners being mixed on a vehicle assembly line
  • Incorrect fastener installation could result in quality defects, rework, and potential safety concerns
  • Leveraged assembly line layout data and fastener specifications to identify locations vulnerable to mix-up events

Key Results

  • Developed a Python-based analysis tool to identify assembly stations vulnerable to fastener mix-ups
  • Identified 60+ stations at elevated risk across the production line
  • Automated an analysis process that previously required extensive manual review
  • Collaborated with production, assembly planning, and technology support teams
  • Enabled proactive defect prevention efforts before issues reached production

My Role

Gathering Data

  • Extracted station-level part usage data from assembly line layout software
  • Merged assembly data with fastener specifications from a master parts database using part numbers
  • Cleaned and filtered datasets to retain critical fastener characteristics for analysis

Analysis

  • Developed criteria to classify fasteners as potentially interchangeable or visually similar
  • Evaluated attributes including fastener type, dimensions, installation method, and line location
  • Implemented an algorithm to compare all fasteners within a configurable station proximity window along the assembly line

Application & Output

  • Processed assembly line layout data through the analysis tool
  • Generated station-level risk reports highlighting vulnerable locations
  • Provided associated part numbers and fastener pairings for engineering review
  • Supported manufacturing teams in prioritizing preventive actions
