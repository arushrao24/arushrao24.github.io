Schlieren Imaging System

GT Advanced Manufacturing Pilot Facility

Overview

  • Visualize behavior of Argon carrier gas during Directed Energy Deposition (DED) metal additive manufacturing (AM)
  • Schlieren imaging technique visualizes changes in refractive index: density, temperature, composition
  • Provide insight into pressure and flow inconsistencies that can possibly affect print quality
  • A custom, rotatable system to install inside DED printers was developed

Key Results

  • Designed and manufactured critical components for optical imagery system
  • Translated experimental requirements into manufactured hardware, factoring in constraints
  • Conducted tests on DED system, gathering important information for further analysis
  • Gained knowledge of Schlieren imaging and optical systems
  • Gained experience working with industrial DED additive manufacturing systems

My Role

Design & Prototyping

  • Designed multiple custom mounts and adapters in SolidWorks
  • Identified system requirements: stability, adjustability, and manufacturability
  • Used DFM and DFA principles to standardize hardware, simplify manufacturing, and improve alignment
  • Created 3D printed prototypes to validate designs prior to manufacturing

Manufacturing & Integration

  • Machined certain components using bandsaws and mills
  • Produced manufacturing-ready drawings for all system components
  • Produced additional documentation for future reference
  • Supported machining a rig to adapt Schlieren platform to a second DED AM system, fitting in tight geometric constraints

Setup, Research, and Testing

  • Calibrated critical system components: light souce, mirrors, knife edge, and camera
  • Optimized components orientation and positioning for best view with minimal optical distortion
  • Conducted experiments on Optomec LENS system across multiple nozzle geometries, angles, and operating environments (air and argon)
  • Captured and organized video footage for further analysis
